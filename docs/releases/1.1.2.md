Shelf can now update itself from inside the app.

- Find updates from the menu bar or Settings > About.
- Check automatically while Shelf runs, without interrupting other apps.
- Choose when to download and install. Active work and unsaved edits are protected before restart.
- Update feeds and downloads are verified before installation.
- Clipboard history, capture choices, folders and tags stay on your Mac.

Install this release once manually if your current build does not have an updater.
